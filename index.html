DrivePod

Harvests YouTube channels into the shared library. Full player with play, pause, seek, and skip — you control exactly what plays and when.

FrogRock

Live broadcast from the same library. No play or pause — it just plays. Every listener hears the same track at the same time, like real radio.

GrabbyTube

Snag single tracks from YouTube to fill gaps between DrivePod's channel harvests — mix-in tracks that keep the playlist flowing.
